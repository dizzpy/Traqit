# Story 18.2: Pricing: Three Tiers, Free Only — Marketing & Pricing

## Story

As a visitor,
I want to see all plans and prices,
so that I know what's free now and what's coming later.

**Status:** ✅ Verified implemented (code-inspected, 2026-10-07). Story written after the fact so QA has a checklist.
**SRS:** FR-18.2 in `docs/srs.md`

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Given the pricing section, when viewed, then Free, Pro and Max cards are shown with prices in the selected currency (USD or LKR).
2. Given the currency switch, when toggled, then prices change: Free $0 / Rs. 0, Pro $0.99 / Rs. 324, Max $1.99 / Rs. 652.
3. Given the Free card, when its button is clicked, then the visitor goes to sign-up.
4. Given the Pro and Max cards, when viewed, then they are visibly disabled, labelled "Coming soon", and their buttons do nothing.
