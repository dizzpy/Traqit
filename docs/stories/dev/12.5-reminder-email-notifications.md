# Story 12.5: Reminder Email Notifications — Settings, Profile & Notifications

## Story

As a job seeker with upcoming interviews,
I want to receive a reminder email before a scheduled pipeline stage,
so that I don't forget an upcoming interview.

**Status:** ✅ Implemented on `fix/storie-issues` (2026-10-07), awaiting QA.
**SRS:** FR-12.5, FR-12.6 in `docs/srs.md`

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Given a pipeline stage with a scheduled date and the user's configured lead time (1/3/24/48 hours), when that lead time is reached, then a reminder email is sent.
2. Given a user has `remindersEnabled: false`, when the check runs, then no email is sent to them.
3. Given a reminder already sent for a stage, when the check runs again, then it is not sent a second time.

## Dev Notes

- `remindersEnabled` and `reminderLeadTime` are already fully wired on the data/UI side — this story is purely about building the actual sending mechanism that was missing. [Source: `docs/srs.md` FR-12.5]
- Cron frequency should be frequent enough to honor short lead times (e.g. 1 hour) meaningfully — a daily-only check would make short lead-time options misleading. Confirm final interval before implementation.

## Implementation Notes

- `/api/cron/reminder-check` (same auth, mailer and schedule as 8.2).

- Skips stages that aren't UPCOMING and applications that are Rejected or Withdrawn.
- **Known limitation:** stage dates are date-only (00:00 UTC), so lead time counts back from midnight UTC on that date. Fixed by story 3.5.
