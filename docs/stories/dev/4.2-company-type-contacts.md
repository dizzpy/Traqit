# Story 4.2: Company Type Contacts — Contacts

## Story

As a job seeker without an individual contact's details,
I want a Person/Company toggle when adding a contact,
so that I can log a generic company inbox/careers contact when I don't have a specific person.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Given the Add Contact form, when I toggle to Company, then the form shows company-appropriate fields (company name, general email, phone, website/careers link) instead of person fields.
2. Given a Company contact saved, when viewed in the Contacts tab, then it's visually distinguishable from a Person contact (icon/layout).
3. Given a Company contact form, when Company is selected, then LinkedIn and Role fields are not required (per product decision).

## Tasks / Subtasks

  - [ ] Add Person/Company toggle above the contact form fields, per reference screenshot
  - [ ] Swap visible fields based on toggle state
- [ ] Schema

  - [ ] Update contacts list rendering to show type-appropriate layout/icon
