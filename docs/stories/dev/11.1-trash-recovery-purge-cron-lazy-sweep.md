# Story 11.1: Trash Recovery Purge Cron Lazy Sweep — Trash

## Story

As a job seeker who deleted something by mistake,
I want a 30-day window to recover deleted applications and email templates,
so that accidental deletes aren't permanent.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Given a soft-deleted application or email template, when I visit Trash within 30 days, then I can restore it.
2. Given an item older than 30 days in Trash, when the daily cron or a page load triggers purge, then it is permanently hard-deleted.

## Tasks / Subtasks

- [ ] Verify recovery + purge
  - [ ] Confirm `/trash`, `/trash/restore`, `/trash/purge` routes
  - [ ] Confirm the `pg_cron` job `purge-trash-daily` is live
  - [ ] Confirm the lazy in-app sweep on Trash page load also purges expired items

## Dependencies

**Prerequisites:** 2.1, 10.1
**Blocks:** 12.3
