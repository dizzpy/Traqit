# Story 17.1: Personal Analytics Dashboard — Analytics

## Story

As a student job hunting,
I want to see my response, interview and offer rates and which sources work best,
so that I can focus my effort where it pays off.

**Status:** ✅ Verified implemented (code-inspected, 2026-10-07). Story written after the fact so QA has a checklist.
**SRS:** FR-17.1 in `docs/srs.md`

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Given my applications, when I open Analytics, then I see response rate, interview rate, offer rate and average days to first response, all calculated over non-Saved applications.
2. Given my applications, when I open Analytics, then I see a status breakdown and applications per week for the last 12 weeks.
3. Given sources with at least 2 applications, when I open Analytics, then each shows its response rate, highest first; sources with fewer than 2 are hidden.
4. Given no applications, when I open Analytics, then I see a helpful empty state, not broken charts or NaN.

## Dev Notes

- Response = not Saved/Applied/Ghosted ÷ non-Saved. Interview = In Progress + Offer + Accepted ÷ non-Saved. Offer = Offer + Accepted ÷ non-Saved.
- Everything is computed from the fetched applications; there's no separate analytics store.
