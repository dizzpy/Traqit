# Story 18.1: Marketing Landing and Legal Pages — Marketing & Pricing

## Story

As a student who hasn't signed up yet,
I want a clear landing page and legal pages,
so that I can understand what Traqit does and trust it before signing up.

**Status:** ✅ Verified implemented (code-inspected, 2026-10-07). Story written after the fact so QA has a checklist.
**SRS:** FR-18.1 in `docs/srs.md`

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Given a signed-out visitor, when they open `/`, then they see the hero, how it works, features, pricing, FAQ and founder memo, with the footer.
2. Given a signed-out visitor, when they open `/privacy`, `/terms` or `/cookies`, then the page loads without sign-in.
3. Given the hero CTA, when clicked, then it leads to `/login`.
